Voice Support Triage

A lightweight ElevenLabs voice workflow that checks mock SaaS account data and either explains the next step or creates a structured engineering escalation.

CUST-101 Healthy account, retry guidance.
CUST-102 Missing entitlement, escalate to Engineering.
CUST-103 Inactive subscription, no engineering escalation.

Use the voice widget to test the customer experience. The backend exposes the mock account data through FastAPI and only creates escalations when the rules indicate a technical issue.

The voice widget connects to the published ElevenLabs agent. The project intentionally keeps the frontend simple and the architecture clear.
